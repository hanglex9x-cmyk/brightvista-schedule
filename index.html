How to view your teaching schedule

Your lessons are on one web page that updates automatically whenever the admin changes the schedule. You only view it; you never need to edit anything.

Open my schedule →
1 · Open the page2 · Weekly schedule3 · Daily grid4 · Colour key

1Open the page

  1. Click Open my schedule above (or the link the admin sent you).
  2. Sign in with any Google account (Gmail) if asked.
  3. Wait 5–10 seconds: the page reads the whole schedule each time.
  4. The first time, a grey bar says “This application was created by a Google Apps Script user”. This is normal; click X to close it.
  5. Bookmark the page so you can open it quickly before every class.
!

If you see a Google Drive page saying the file cannot be opened, open the link in a private / incognito window and sign in with only one Google account.

2Weekly schedule

All lessons of the week, grouped by day. This is the first screen you see.

  1. Week from Monday: pick any date to jump to that week.
  2. ◀ ▶: previous / next week.
  3. Teacher: choose your name to see only your lessons.
  4. search student: type a name to find one student.
  5. A coloured row is a special lesson, here a Test 1. See the colour key.
  6. Lesson 12/30 means lesson 12 of 30. Min is the lesson length in minutes.
✓

Tip: choose your name in Teacher first; the page keeps the list short and easy to read on a phone.

3Daily grid

One day as a grid: one column per teacher, one row per 30 minutes.

  1. Day: pick a date.
  2. ◀ ▶: previous / next day (Sunday is skipped).
  3. Find your column by your name and colour. Scroll sideways to see more teachers.
  4. White cells are your working hours, grey cells are outside them. A student name in a cell is a lesson at that time.

Student names are blurred in these pictures for privacy; you will see them on the real page.
